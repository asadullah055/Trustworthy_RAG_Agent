Acme Learning Refund Policy

Students can request a refund within 14 calendar days of purchase if they have completed less than 20 percent of the course material.

Refund requests must include the purchase email, order number, and reason for the request.

Refunds are not available for cohort-based live workshops after the first live session has started.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.
